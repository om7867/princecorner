Their Problems (You Solve These)

Why Prince Corner Needs Your System — Even If They Don't Know It Yet

📊

No Centralized Control Over 11 Outlets

The owner sits at Isanpur but has zero real-time visibility into what's selling at Gota vs Bopal vs Satellite. Which branch is underperforming? Which items are wasted? He's running blind across 11 locations.

⭐

Inconsistent Quality Across Branches

Isanpur has 4.0 rating but Vastrapur has 2.7 with complaints about food quality. Without centralized menu + inventory control, each branch does its own thing. Reviews mention "not fresh" food at some outlets.

🕐

Crowding & Wait Times = Lost Customers

Bopal branch reviews say "crowded most of the times, you have to wait." QR table ordering eliminates the waiter bottleneck — customers order the second they sit down.

💸

Paying 25-30% Commission to Zomato/Swiggy

On every online order, Zomato/Swiggy takes 25-30% cut. On ₹400 average order, that's ₹100-120 gone per order. His own website with ordering = 0% commission on direct orders.

🚫

No Customer Data Ownership

Zomato owns the customer. Prince Corner has zero data — no phone numbers, no order history, no repeat customer identification. He can't run his own promotions or loyalty programs.

📱

No Online Presence Beyond Aggregators

Prince Corner has only an Instagram (368 followers) and third-party listings. No website, no direct ordering. They're 100% dependent on platforms they don't control.

Approach Strategy

How to Approach Him — Step by Step

01

Don't "Sell" — Start as a Conversation Through Your Dad

Have your dad casually bring it up: "My son has built something for restaurants, he wanted your opinion on it as someone who runs 11 branches. Can he show you for 10 minutes?" This frames you as someone seeking guidance, not selling. Restaurant owners respect that. Ask your dad to set up a chai meeting at the Isanpur branch — his home turf where he's comfortable.

02

Go at the Right Time

Visit between 3:00 PM – 5:00 PM (after lunch rush, before dinner). Never during peak hours. Weekdays are better — Tuesday/Wednesday/Thursday. The owner will be relaxed and have bandwidth to listen. Dress sharp-casual, carry a tablet or laptop with your demo ready.

03

Open With Praise + A Problem He Recognizes

Start by genuinely appreciating what he's built — 11 outlets from an Isanpur street food corner is impressive. Then ask a question he can't dodge: "Uncle, with 11 branches, how do you track what's selling at each outlet daily? Do you get reports on your phone?" — He'll admit he doesn't have real-time data. That's your opening.

04

Show the Demo on His Own Table

Literally scan a QR code on the table you're sitting at. Show how a customer would see the Prince Corner menu, customize an order, and place it — no waiter needed. Show him the admin panel with live orders, branch-wise sales, and the super-admin dashboard. Make it about HIS brand, not your software.

05

Hit the Money Argument

Do quick math together: "If even 50 orders a day come directly through your website instead of Zomato, at ₹400 average, that's ₹20,000/day × 30 days = ₹6 lakh/month. Zomato takes 25% = ₹1.5 lakh saved per month. My entire system costs less than what you pay Zomato in one week."

06

Offer a Zero-Risk Pilot

Propose: "Let's try it at just one branch — Isanpur — for 1 month. I'll set everything up. If it doesn't work, you owe nothing. If you see value, we roll it to other branches." Zero risk = no reason to say no.

Objection Handling

Every "No" He'll Say — And Your Reply

"Zomato aur Swiggy hai na, website ki kya zarurat?"
Zomato is a discovery platform, not YOUR platform. Zomato owns the customer — you don't even get their phone number. They can increase commission anytime (they already went from 18% to 25-28%). And when a customer searches "pav bhaji near me" on Zomato, they also see your competitors. On YOUR website, it's only Prince Corner. Plus, every repeat customer who orders directly saves you ₹100+ per order. You're not removing Zomato — you're adding a channel where YOU keep 100% of the money.
"QR se order kaun karega? Log waiter ko bulate hai"
Café Coffee Day, McDonald's, Domino's, KFC — sab QR use kar rahe hain. Young customers (18-30) actually PREFER scanning and ordering themselves. They don't want to wait for a waiter, don't want to repeat the order twice. And for YOU — it means fewer waiters needed, fewer wrong orders, faster table turnover. One waiter handles 10 tables instead of 5. Your Bopal branch reviews say "always crowded, have to wait" — QR ordering fixes that instantly.
"Mere staff ko technology nahi aati"
The system is designed for that. Kitchen staff just see orders appearing on a screen — like WhatsApp messages. No typing, no learning. I'll personally train your staff at each branch. And the admin panel is in simple Gujarati/Hindi-friendly language. Even your Isanpur counter boy can use it — I'll make sure of it.
"Kitna kharcha aayega? Bahut mehnga hoga"
The QR ordering system costs about ₹2,000-3,000 per month per branch — that's less than one day's Zomato commission. The website costs a one-time setup fee. And I'm offering the first month completely free as a trial at Isanpur. If it doesn't add value, you stop — zero loss. But I'm confident that in one month, you'll want it at all 11 branches.
"Abhi chal raha hai, change ki kya zarurat?"
Everything was "chal raha hai" before Zomato too — people still ordered on phone. But restaurants that got on Zomato early got the advantage. Similarly, restaurants that set up their own direct ordering NOW will save lakhs when commission rates increase further. Your competitors are already doing this. Barbeque Nation, Honest Restaurant — all have their own ordering. At 11 branches, you're big enough to deserve your own system.
"Mujhe sochne do / I'll think about it"
Don't push. Say: "Bilkul uncle, take your time. Main ek kaam karta hoon — aapke Isanpur branch ka demo ready karke rakh deta hoon, Prince Corner ke menu ke saath. Jab bhi 15 minute mile, I'll show you on your own phone how it looks. No commitment." Then follow up after 3-4 days via your dad, not directly. Keep it warm, not pushy.

Before the Meeting

Your Preparation Checklist

🎨

Customize the Demo

Put Prince Corner's actual menu in your demo — Butter Bhaji Pav, Pizza Dosa, Mysore Masala Dosa, Chinese Bhel, Manchurian Noodles. Use their logo and orange brand color. When he sees HIS brand in the system, it becomes real.

📱

Print 2-3 QR Codes

Laminate them. Place one on the table when you sit down. The physical QR code makes it tangible — he can hold it, flip it, imagine it on his tables. Show, don't just tell.

📄

One-Page Summary

Print a clean one-pager: What it does, 3 benefits, pricing, your contact. Leave it with him. He'll look at it later and show his family/partner. Keep it Gujarati-friendly if possible.

🔢

ROI Calculation Sheet

Pre-calculate the Zomato commission savings math for Prince Corner specifically. Show: current commission loss vs. direct ordering savings. Concrete numbers beat vague promises every time.

Mindset

Final Tips That Matter

Speak Gujarati / Hindi — Not Tech English

Don't say "ERP", "SaaS", "subscription model", "dashboard". Say "sabhi branch ka hisaab ek jagah", "mahine ka plan", "phone pe live dekhna". Match his language, not yours.

Respect That He Built This Without Tech

He grew from 1 to 11 branches without any software. Don't imply he's behind the times. Frame it as: "You've built something amazing. This technology will help you scale it to 20-25 branches without the headaches doubling."

First Meeting Goal: Not a Sale — A Second Meeting

Your ONLY goal in the first meeting is to get him to say "Interesting, show me more" or "Set it up at one branch and let me try." Don't try to close the deal on day one. Plant the seed, let it grow.

Follow Up Through Your Dad, Not Cold Calls

The relationship channel (your dad's friendship) is your biggest advantage. After the meeting, let your dad casually mention it: "Mera beta bol raha tha aapko uss system mein interest tha?" This keeps it warm without being pushy.

You've Got This 💪

You have the research, the script, the objection handling, and the pricing. Now customize your demo with Prince Corner's menu, practice the script 3 times out loud, and book the meeting. The product sells itself — you just need to get it in front of him.
